    Hands-on.

    Answer each of the following as best you can. Feel free to discuss your thoughts with peers around you.
    🔗
    🔗

    1.

    Suppose we want to evaluate the infinite sum \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{5}{4n^2 - 1}\ \) (not just whether it converges)
    🔗

    (a)

    Find the partial fraction decomposition (including coefficients) for \(\dfrac{5}{4n^2 - 1}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Use your answer from part (a) to write a formula for \(s_n\text{,}\) the \(n\)-th partial sum of the series.
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Use your answer from part (b) to evaluate the infinite sum.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Evaluate the following improper integral, or show that it diverges:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int_{-\infty}^{0} x e^{2x}\,dx \end{equation*}
    🔗

    3.

    Compute the integral
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int \frac{x^2}{(9 - x^2)^{3/2}}\,dx \end{equation*}
    🔗

    4.

    Evaluate the following improper integral, or show that it diverges:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int_{2}^{\infty} \left(\frac{7}{7x+2} - \frac{4}{4x+1}\right) dx \end{equation*}
    🔗

    5.

    Compute the integral
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + 4x}}\,dx \end{equation*}
    (Hint: you may want to complete the square in the denominator)
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    Compute the integral
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int \frac{3x^3 + 6x + 9}{x^4 + 3x^2}\,dx \end{equation*}
    🔗

    7.

    Determine whether the series below converges or diverges:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(\ln(n))^3} \end{equation*}
    🔗

    8.

    Compute
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \int \frac{\sec^4(x)}{\tan(x)}\,dx \end{equation*}
