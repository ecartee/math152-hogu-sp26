    Comparison Theorem.

    Suppose that \(f\) and \(g\) are continuous functions with \(f(x) \geq g(x) \geq 0\) for \(x \geq a\text{.}\)
    🔗
    (a) If \(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx\) is convergent, then \(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{\infty} g(x)\,dx\) is convergent.
    🔗
    (b) If \(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{\infty} g(x)\,dx\) is divergent, then \(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx\) is divergent.
