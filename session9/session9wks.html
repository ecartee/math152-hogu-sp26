    4.

    Note: The geometric series formula is \(\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n = \frac{a}{1-r}\) and this series converges for \(|r| \lt 1\text{.}\)
    🔗
    We can use this to help find power series representations for certain functions, for instance \(f(x) = \dfrac{1}{1-x} = \displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x^n\)
    🔗

    (a)

    Use the above information to find a power series representation for
    \begin{equation*} g(x) = \frac{1}{2x + 3} \end{equation*}
    and determine its radius of convergence.
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Now find a power series representation for
    \begin{equation*} h(x) = \frac{2}{4 + x^2} \end{equation*}
    and determine its radius of convergence.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Find the power series representation for
    \begin{equation*} f(x) = \frac{x}{2x^2 + 3} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    Hint: \(\dfrac{x}{2x^2 + 3} = \dfrac{x}{3} \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{1 + \frac{2}{3}x^2}\right)\)
